RULES & STANDARDS

A fair play policy and scoring systems guidelines for tournament matches.

Matches are organized in a standard single-elimination bracket or league points format depending on the event size:

  • Solo/Duo Matches: 3 qualifying rounds, top 4 from each room advance to the final lobby.
  • Squad Matches: Group stages of 12 teams per lobby. Best of 3 maps (Bermuda, Purgatory, Kalahari) with overall points determining the top 3 finalists.

Points are awarded based on both placement standings and overall kills. Each kill yields 1 point.

PLACEMENT POINTS PLACEMENT POINTS
1st Place (Booyah!) 12 pts 6th - 10th Place 2 pts
2nd Place 9 pts 11th - 15th Place 1 pt
3rd Place 7 pts 16th+ Place 0 pts
4th Place 5 pts Kill Points 1 pt / Kill
5th Place 4 pts - -

Standard mapping cycles across matches are predefined to test team adaptability:

  • Match 1: Bermuda (Classic BR)
  • Match 2: Purgatory (High Altitude Tactics)
  • Match 3: Kalahari (Desert Combat)

Our platform uses standard Garena anti-cheat measures. All forms of third-party modifications, scripts, macros, or gameplay exploits are strictly forbidden.

Reporting: Captains must capture and upload clear recording clips to report hackers within 30 minutes of match completion.

Disqualification triggers immediately under these circumstances:

  • Teammates sharing accounts or letting external players match under registered credentials.
  • Failure to join the game room within the 10-minute lobby countdown grace period.
  • Toxic behavior, verbal abuse, or bad sportsmanship in global chats.

CRITICAL DIRECTIVES

Lobby room codes and slots keys are exclusively visible on the Match Schedule panel to registered and validated teams. Please ensure your players keep the keys private. Sharing lobby codes outside your squad will result in automatic match forfeit.

CONTACT ARBITRATOR

Have questions regarding points, disputes, or payment validation? Write to the support admins.
